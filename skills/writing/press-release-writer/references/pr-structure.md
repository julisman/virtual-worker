# Press release structure

The canonical section order with format rules and a filled example skeleton. Follow
the order exactly; omit a section only if the user says so.

## Section order

1. **Release line** — `FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE` (or `EMBARGOED UNTIL <date, time, timezone>`).
2. **Headline** — one line, present tense, the news up front. ≤ ~90 characters.
   No exclamation marks, no unsourced superlatives.
3. **Subheadline** (optional) — one sentence, the strongest supporting fact or number.
4. **Dateline + lede** — `CITY, Country — Month D, YYYY —` then the lede paragraph
   answering who / what / when / why-it-matters in ≤ ~40 words.
   Indonesian format: `JAKARTA, 17 Agustus 2026 —`.
5. **Body** — 2–4 paragraphs, inverted pyramid (most important first):
   - details and concrete numbers of the announcement;
   - availability, pricing, rollout dates;
   - context: the problem it addresses, market background the user supplied.
6. **Quotes** — 1–2, each its own paragraph, placed after the first or second body
   paragraph is also acceptable. Format:
   `"<quote>," said <Full Name>, <Title> at <Company>. "<optional second sentence>."`
   Executive quote = vision/why; second quote (partner/customer) = outside validation.
   In drafts, prefix each with `[DRAFT QUOTE — needs approval from <name>]`.
7. **Boilerplate** — `About <Company>`: 2–4 sentences, written in third person,
   reusable verbatim across releases. Ends with the company URL.
8. **Contact block**:
   ```
   Media contact:
   <Name>, <Title>
   <email>
   <phone, optional>
   ```
9. **End mark** — `###` centered on its own line (journalistic end-of-release signal).

## Style rules

- Third person throughout the body ("the company", never "we") — "we" lives only
  inside quotes.
- One idea per paragraph; paragraphs 2–4 sentences.
- Spell out the company name on first use; short form after.
- First reference to a person: full name + title; after that, surname.
- Total length: 300–500 words (excluding boilerplate/contact).
- Active voice; no jargon a general business journalist wouldn't know.

## Example skeleton (structure only — never copy the values)

```
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

Acme Robotics Launches Field-Repair Robot for Palm Plantations

New unit cuts equipment downtime by up to 40% in early trials

JAKARTA, Indonesia — March 3, 2026 — Acme Robotics, a maker of agricultural
maintenance robots, today launched FieldFix, a self-navigating repair unit for
palm-oil plantations, available in Indonesia and Malaysia from April 2026.

[body paragraph: details + numbers]

[DRAFT QUOTE — needs approval from Siti Rahma] "…," said Siti Rahma, CEO of
Acme Robotics. "…"

[body paragraph: availability, pricing]

About Acme Robotics
[2–4 sentences + URL]

Media contact:
[Name], [Title]
[email]

###
```
